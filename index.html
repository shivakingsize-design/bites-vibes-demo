About Becafe

A neighbourhood café built around the cup.

Becafe is a small coffee shop in Chanchalguda, Hyderabad, designed as a place to meet, study and take a break from the day. The focus is on coffee, cold drinks and simple food that pairs well with them.

The room is warm and quiet enough for conversation or work, with the menu kept deliberately focused so every drink and bite gets attention.
